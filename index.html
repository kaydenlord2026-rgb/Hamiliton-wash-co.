👻 Common questions

A few things people ask before they book.

Can I book windows on their own?

Yes. Windows can be booked alone or together with washing for driveways and walkways.

Will pressure washing damage my driveway or walkway?

Not when the pressure is matched to the surface. Concrete, interlock and asphalt each get the pressure and technique that suits them.

Do I need to be home?

Usually not. We need access to the areas being worked on, plus an outdoor water tap for washing jobs.

When is the best time of year?

Washing works best from spring through fall, when temperatures stay above freezing. Snow removal runs through the winter.
